About
The person behind the projects.

I have always believed AI should help people, not replace them. That belief is behind everything I build.

I started in product and merchandising analytics, learning how data shapes real decisions. At Lenskart and Meesho, I saw how the right insight at the right time changes what a team does next. That got me obsessed with automation — not automation for its own sake, but automation that gives people back their time.

At OMG23, I worked directly with the Disney client. I built systems that eliminated manual reporting entirely and freed the team to actually think. Leadership adopted it as the standard. That moment showed me what AI-powered analytics can actually do.

Now I build AI agents. Tools that diagnose pipeline failures, analyze payments data, detect cancer risk. Real things that work. Always learning. Always shipping. Always keeping the human touch.
